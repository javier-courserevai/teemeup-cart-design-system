# ChatBubble

Bob's replies sit left on `muted` under his name; the golfer's words sit right on purple.

- One idea per bubble, 15px, at most four lines. Longer answers become an `AnswerCard`.
- Bob talks in the first person and uses the golfer's first name: “Welcome, Sam! You're on the 9:40 tee time.”
- Emoji are allowed only in Bob's bubbles, and sparingly.
