# ScoreCard

Nine holes of the group's scores: a row per hole, a column per golfer, par beside the hole number.

- Up to four golfers: this cart's two (names in purple) and the other cart of the group, synced and read-only.
- Golf's own marks: under par circled in green, two or more over boxed; par and bogey plain. Holes not played show a dash; the current hole is tinted.
- The foot gives each golfer's total for the nine and the score against par for the holes played (“E”, “+2”).
- Front 9 and Back 9 are switched with a `SegmentedControl` above it. Opened from Menu → Score, as a step with a back button.
