# BottomBar

The 80px bar at the foot of every round screen; Talk to Bob is always its widest item.

- MVP (`items="mvp"`, the default): Talk to Bob and Help.
- Phase 3 (`items="full"`): Bob, Food, Music, Help, with the labels shortened to fit.
- Talk to Bob is the one purple fill on the screen; the rest are `muted` pills.
- Inner screens with their own actions (the answer, booking, staff) replace the bar with one or two block `Button`s in the same 80px band.
