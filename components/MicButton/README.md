# MicButton

Push-to-talk for Bob: a 120px purple mic you hold to speak and release to send, or tap to start and tap again to send.

- Ready: “Hold to talk, or tap”. Listening: live bars above the mic, a purple halo, “Listening… release to send”.
- While listening, music (phase 3) drops to 20% and the screen hides everything but the mic and the transcript.
- With no signal the mic is disabled and the hint says why: “Bob needs signal to answer. Yardages still work.”
