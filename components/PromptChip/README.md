# PromptChip

A suggested question or follow-up Bob offers, one tap to send.

- **Conversation starters** (`block`): full-width rows aligned with the screen edges, grouped just above the chat bar under a “Try asking” label. Each row has an icon tile for the topic, the question in bold without quotes (“How far to the pin?”), and a send arrow on the right. Never indented under Bob's bubble.
- **Follow-ups** after an answer use the same `block` row right under it (“Show the drop zone on the map”).
- The compact pill (no `block`) is for one quick reply inline, such as Stop while Bob listens.
- At least 56px tall (rows 60px). Two or three at a time, never more.
