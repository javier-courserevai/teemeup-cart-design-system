# Banner

A full-width strip under the header for a condition that lasts: no signal, play suspended, low battery, all clear, out of a zone.

- Icon plus one or two sentences. First words say what it is; the rest says what still works or what to do.
- `offline`: “No signal. Yardages still work. Lightning status is unavailable, so watch the sky.”
- `suspended` stays after the golfer acknowledges lightning, until the all clear.
- Colours are the fixed safety set; they are the same by day and at dusk.
