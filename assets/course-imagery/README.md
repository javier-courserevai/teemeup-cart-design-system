# Course imagery

`waterford-hole.jpg`: one hole at Waterford Golf Club, Rock Hill, SC (the pilot), for `HoleMap`'s `image` prop.

- Source: USGS NAIP Imagery, The National Map (USGS / USDA), 0.6 m per pixel. Public domain; credit shown as "Imagery: USGS NAIP".
- Rotated tee-to-green (green at the top) and cropped to 656 × 824 px, 2× the 328 × 412 map space. About 1 yard per map unit.
- Hole measured off the photo: about 357 yds tee to green. Its number on the scorecard is not confirmed yet.
- Map geometry used on the canvas: pin (164, 54), cart (100, 380), tap point (165, 200), zone `M108 92L206 88L210 122L104 126Z`.
